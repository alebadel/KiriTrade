The question and the method

Importers record tuna as Kiribati-origin by the flag of the catching vessel. The balance of payments counts a sale as a Kiribati export only if the vessel's operator is resident in Kiribati. True exports are the FOB value (at the port of loading, without freight and insurance) of tuna sold to non-residents by operators resident in Kiribati, whatever the flag or the route, whether or not any statistic recorded it. A catch by a vessel run from abroad is not a Kiribati export. The Kiribati National Statistics Office measures this quantity inside its goods-exports line; the estimate here is a second reading from different evidence, offered to help that work.

Thirty AI agents (the "experts") work alone on the same evidence pack and instructions. Each agent is one fresh session of a large language model, with no memory of the other sessions. In order, each:

  1. writes numbered principles that connect the evidence to the question, and freezes them;
  2. walks from the raw partner figure to its estimate in six signed layers, each with a range and a reason;
  3. audits its own answer.

A program checks every answer (layers reconcile, ranges ordered, no banned words) and computes the statistics shown here; the agents do no arithmetic that is published. The spread across the thirty is the room for judgment the evidence leaves. It is not a standard error: the agents share one evidence pack and one model, so what none of them knows does not show. Terms are defined in the glossary.

          What this is, and is not

          It is a stated theory, written down before the numbers, applied without exception to the evidence by thirty independent readers, with the disagreement shown. Every layer has a reason, and every reason cites the evidence it rests on.

          It is not an official estimate, and the official figure is not used to score it: the two answer the same question from different evidence, and the estimate can sit above or below the published figure without either being wrong. Whether the agents understand how a published figure is built is a separate test, designed but not yet run, on the Validation page.
