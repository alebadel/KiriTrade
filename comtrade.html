    How complete the data are

    Partners publish on their own timetables, so recent periods fill in gradually. The chart shows, for each period, how much of the partner panel has published. Read every other chart with this one in mind.

    The partner panel

    What partners record: exports and imports

    These are the sums of what every partner on the panel declared about its trade with Kiribati, grouped by product. Kiribati's own total is drawn as a line where it has been published. Lighter bars mean fewer than 60% of partners have published for that period, so the figure will still grow.

      What we learn

      Input 1 of 5 · Next: Ship tracking →

      Early estimate of the partner series itself

      A different, smaller question: what will importers record for months they have not yet published? Thailand, the main buyer, publishes with a lag of several months. A statistical baseline fills the gap from what has been published, and the agents of an earlier run gave their own view of the same total. This is an estimate of the flag-basis figure, not of true exports.

      Sources. UN Comtrade (partner returns and Kiribati's own returns); IMF balance-of-payments statistics; the Kiribati National Statistics Office through Comtrade.
