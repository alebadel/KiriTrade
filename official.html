Official statistics (Kiribati)

Kiribati's own published trade figures: the balance of payments and the customs returns. Input 4 of 5 on the home page.

Shown for comparison. The inference does not try to match them; the agents read them as evidence of what the compiler recorded.

What these figures are

RecordCompiled byRuleTimingUse here
Balance of payments, goods exports and importsKiribati National Statistics Office with the IMFIMF Balance of Payments Manual (BPM6): a good is an export when its ownership passes from a resident to a non-resident, valued at the border (FOB)Annual; latest year an estimateReference line beside the partner data and the agents' estimate
Customs returns, by product and monthKiribati customs office, published to UN ComtradeGoods recorded leaving and entering the countryMonthly, for the months processed; gaps in some yearsMonths-published check on the balance of payments

For fish caught at sea, the balance of payments follows the residence of the vessel's operator, not the vessel's flag; importing countries follow the flag. That is why the goods-exports line here is small next to the tuna that partners record as Kiribati-origin on the Partner data page. The two answer different questions.

Fishing access fees paid by foreign fleets are income in the balance of payments, a separate flow from goods. They are not shown here and are not compared with the tuna series on this site.

Balance of payments: goods exports and imports

Customs returns published to UN Comtrade

Kiribati's own monthly returns. The number of months published differs by year, so each year is scaled to 12 months (value × 12 / months published) to be comparable with the annual balance of payments.

The full comparison of official figures with partner records, including imports and the months each year covers, is in the Comparison with the official figures section of the Partner data page.
