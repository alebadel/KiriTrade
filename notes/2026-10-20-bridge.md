# Run bridge-v2: from the partner figure to true exports, 2023 to 2025 — 20 October 2026

Thirty AI agents, each in a fresh context with the same evidence pack and the same instructions (`tracker/agents/bridge_prompt.md`), each walked from the raw partner-reported tuna figure to its own estimate of Kiribati's true tuna exports in six signed layers, for calendar 2023, 2024 and 2025. All thirty answers passed the checks (layers in order, start plus layers equal to the estimate, ranges ordered, no banned words). The statistics below are computed by `tracker/build_inference_data.py` from the thirty files (`data/inference.json`); the agents did no arithmetic that is published.

**Start values** (sum of the monthly partner returns in `data/series.json`, USD millions): 2023 81.77; 2024 97.57; 2025 86.93.

**Estimated true exports, mean of thirty agents** (10th to 90th percentile across agents): 2023 19.5 (12.9 to 28.3); 2024 23.6 (16.5 to 34.3); 2025 32.9 (25.1 to 40.5). As a share of the partner figure: 24, 24 and 38 percent.

**Where the bridge moves.** One layer carries almost all of it: the catch of vessels run by non-resident operators, removed, mean −62.9 (2023), −74.7 (2024), −57.1 (2025). Valuation (CIF to FOB) removes about 5 to 6 in each year. Resident catch coded to another origin adds 3.1 to 4.3; resident sales by unrecorded routes add 3.3 to 5.6; treatment and other adjustments are each under 1 in absolute value. The spread across agents sits in the same layer: for 2025 the 10th to 90th percentile of the non-resident layer runs from −64.6 to −51.1, a width of 13.5, against widths of 1.2 to 3.7 for the other five layers.

**What the official figures did.** The pack held Kiribati's balance-of-payments goods exports (2023 8.1, 2024 8.5, 2025 17.3 estimate, all goods) and customs returns with the months published (tuna 0.0, 0.6 and 3.4 over 7, 10 and 10 months). The agents were told these are evidence of what the compiler recorded, not the target. 26 of the 30 notes describe them as evidence of the compiler's coverage or as a floor or cross-check: resident sales at sea do not pass through customs, so the customs tuna line is read as a lower bound, not the answer (`official_use` in data/inference.json); the other four call them a consistency check or context. The same question put to thirty agents earlier the same day without the official figures (run bridge-v1, 2025 only) gave a mean of 36.4 (28.1 to 45.7); with the official figures in view the 2025 mean is 32.9. The instructions were the same except for the added file and the two extra years.

**What this run cannot tell.** The spread across agents is disagreement given identical evidence and one underlying model; it is not a standard error. The size of the non-resident layer rests on register address hints (a usable owner address for 55 of 116 entries) and the agents' prior knowledge of Pacific purse-seine fleets, not on any observed ownership share. No news, no Kiribati fisheries regulation and no comparison country were in the pack. The validation test (predicting other countries' published figures with the figure withheld) has not been run; the panel is empty.
